How to Use UniversalDrop

Direct offline-first transfer between PC and phone without cloud accounts.

1
Same Local Network: Ensure your phone and PC are connected to the same Wi-Fi, Ethernet, or Mobile Hotspot.
2
Scan QR or Enter PIN: Open your phone camera or browser and point it at the QR code on the screen.
3
Approve & Drop Files: Click "Approve Connection" on this PC. You can now drag and drop files or stream photos instantly!
